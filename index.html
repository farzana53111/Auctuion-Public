How Minthouse works

From consignment to settlement, every lot follows the same vetted path.

01 — Intake

Submit for evaluation

Send photos and details of your item. Our specialists confirm authenticity and estimate value before it's listed.

02 — Auction

List and bid

Lots run on a fixed close with live extensions, so a bid in the final seconds always gets a fair chance to be topped.

03 — Settlement

Ship and get paid

Winning bidders pay through escrow; sellers are paid once the item is verified on arrival.
